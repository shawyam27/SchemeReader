# Schemes

## [Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pm-kisan)
Farmer welfare scheme by Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare to provide income support to all landholding farmers' families in the country to supplement their financial needs for procuring various inputs related to agriculture and allied activities as well as domestic needs. 
## [Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana: Per Drop More Crop](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pmksypdmc)
The scheme “Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana: Per Drop More Crop” was launched by the Dept. of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Govt. of India on 1st July 2015. The scheme mainly focuses on water use efficiency at farm level through Micro Irrigation.
## [Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojna (PMFBY)](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pmfby)
Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana was launched in 2016 with the aim to support farmers by providing an affordable comprehensive risk cover for crops.
## [Netaji Subhas - ICAR International Fellowship](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/ns-icarif)
Introduced in 2009-10, the “Netaji Subhas - ICAR International Fellowship” is a scheme by to develop competent human resource that are trained in the identified best laboratories in the world (for Indian candidates) and similarly expose overseas candidates to the best Agriculture Universities.
## [ICAR Emeritus Scientist](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/icar-es)
The ICAR Emeritus Scientist Scheme is a structural method of tapping the Brain and skill bank of the outstanding superannuated professionals of NARS by allowing them to complete the work in hand for its fruitful conclusion, and utilize their talent in teaching specialized courses. 
## [ICAR Senior Research Fellowship For Post-Graduate Studies In Agricultural Sciences](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/icar-srf-pgs)
ICAR awards a certain number of Senior Research Fellowships for Ph.D. in an Agricultural University (AU), in different disciplines of agriculture and allied sciences. This is done to encourage talented students to undertake higher agricultural education.
## [ICAR Junior Research Fellowship For Post-Graduate Studies In Agricultural Sciences](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/icar-jrf-pgs)
ICAR awards a certain number of Junior Research Fellowships for Postgraduate Studies (course and research work) leading to a Master’s degree, in different disciplines of agriculture and allied sciences. This is done to encourage talented students to undertake higher agricultural education.
## [National Food Security Mission NFSM](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/nfsmcss)
National Food Security Mission was launched in 2007-08 to increase the production of rice, wheat and pulses. Later, coarse cereals, nutri cereals, commercial crops (sugarcane, jute, and cotton) were also added to the mission.
## [Coconut Palm Insurance Scheme](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/cpis)
The “Coconut Palm Insurance Scheme (CPIS)” is being implemented by the Coconut Development Board, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India with the objective of insuring coconut palms against natural calamities, climatic risks, pests, diseases, and other perils. 
## [National Agriculture Market](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/e-nam)
Launched on 14th April 2016, e-NAM is a pan-India electronic trading platform that links existing mandis to create a unified online market for agricultural commodities, promoting “One Nation One Market” and enabling transparent, efficient, and nationwide agri-trade.
