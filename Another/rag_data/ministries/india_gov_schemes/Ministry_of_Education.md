## [Pradhan Mantri Uchchatar Shiksha Protsahan (PM-USP) Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship for College and University students](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/csss-cus)
A scholarship scheme by the Dept. of Higher Education to provide financial assistance to meritorious students from poor families to meet a part of their day-to-day expenses while pursuing higher studies. The scholarships are awarded on the basis of results of Higher Secondary / Class 12th Board Exam
## [Pradhan Mantri Uchchatar Shiksha Protsahan Yojana: Central Sector Interest Subsidy Scheme](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pm-uspycsiss)
The scheme provides full interest subsidy during the moratorium period on loan availed under Model Education Loan Scheme of Indian Banks’ Association (IBA) to students belonging to economically weaker sections whose annual parental income is up to ₹4.5 Lakhs from all sources.
## [PM POSHAN - Prime Minister's Overarching Scheme For Holistic Nourishment](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pm-poshan)
PM POSHAN is a centrally sponsored scheme by the Department of School Education & Literacy, Ministry of Education. Under this scheme, one hot cooked meal will be provided to the children studying in Government and Government – aided schools. The Scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Education.
## [Pradhan Mantri Uchchatar Shiksha Protsahan Yojana - Special Scholarship Scheme for Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pm-usp-sssjkl)
This initiative provides academic fees and maintenance allowances to support students from Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh pursuing higher education outside the Union Territories
## [Pradhan Mantri Vidyalaxmi (PM-Vidyalaxmi) Scheme](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/pmvs)
The "Pradhan Mantri Vidyalaxmi (Pm-vidyalaxmi) Scheme" aims to provide financial support for higher education through education loans. Under this scheme, financial assistance of up to ₹10,00,000/- is provided to meritorious students seeking admission to domestic institutions.
## [ICSSR Doctoral Fellowship](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/doctoral-fellowship)
## [AICTE-LILAVATI AWARD](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/ala)
AICTE-LILAVATI AWARD intends to recognize efforts being made by AICTE-approved institutions to treat women with ‘equality and fairness’ in all spheres of their lives.
## [Research Grant For In-Service Faculty Members](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/rgisfm)
The scheme "Research Grant for In-Service Faculty Members" was introduced by the University Grants Commission (UGC), Department of Higher Education (DoHE). The objective of this research grant is to provide opportunities to regularly appointed faculty members of Universities/ Institutes/ Colleges.
## [Emeritus Fellowship](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/ef)
A fellowship scheme by University Grants Commission to provide an opportunity to superannuated teachers to pursue active research in their respective field of specialization in Indian Universities/Colleges/ Institutions.
## [Savitribai Jyotirao Phule Fellowship For Single Girl Child](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/schemes/sjpfsgc)
Savitribai Jyotirao Phule Fellowship for Single Girl Child (SJSGC) is a Fellowship Scheme by the University Grants Commission (UGC), Department of Higher Education, for pursuing research work leading to the award of a Ph.D. degree. The target group of this scheme is the ‘single girl child'.
